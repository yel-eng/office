Our Story

Crafting Excellence Since Day One

At Velvet Coffee, we believe every cup tells a story. From the misty highlands of Ethiopia to the volcanic soils of Guatemala, we source only the top 1% of beans worldwide. Our master roasters bring out each bean's unique character through careful, small-batch roasting.

Every drink is handcrafted by our award-winning baristas who treat coffee-making as an art form — ensuring you experience luxury in every single sip.
